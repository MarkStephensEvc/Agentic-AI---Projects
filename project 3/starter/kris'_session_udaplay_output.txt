It was designed for **PC** and is considered one of the most influential turn-based strategy games of its time. This game allowed players to guide their chosen civilizations from the dawn of recorded history through various eras, making strategic decisions that would lead to their civilization's glory or downfall (sources: [2K Official Civilization Site](https://civilization.2k.com/civ), [PC Gamer](https://www.pcgamer.com/the-complete-history-of-civilization)).
